Lucky Bar Slot is a classic fruit machine game.

- Bet on eight fruit and symbol cells, then press START.
- Watch the lights run around the board and see where they stop.
- Trigger bonus cells and win combinations.
- Double your winnings with the BIG or SMALL gamble.
- Look up the full rules in the in-game help.

This is a game for fun. Points have no cash value and no real money is used to play.
